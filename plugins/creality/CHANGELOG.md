# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-20
- First version: a printer tile (state, file, progress, time left and when it is done, temperatures, layer), a camera
  tile (the printer's camera with the progress under it, live on the ESP32-P4 screens) and a control card (pause or
  resume, stop with a second tap to confirm, and the light). Needs the Creality WebSocket integration from HACS (see
  the README).
