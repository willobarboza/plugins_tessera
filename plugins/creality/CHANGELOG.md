# Changelog

## 0.1.1 - 2026-11-24
- The light key on the control card changes at once when tapped, and Home Assistant confirms it, like Tessera's own
  controls. The screen now turns the light on and off instead of toggling it.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-20
- First version: a printer tile (state, file, progress, time left and when it is done, temperatures, layer) and a
  control card (pause or resume, stop with a second tap to confirm, and the light). For the camera, use Tessera's own
  camera tile. Needs the Creality WebSocket integration from HACS (see the README).
